# Ecommerce Product Poster Director

Use for product hero images, commercial posters, premium object renders, packaging visuals, and productized concept images.

Guide tactile product presence, commercial restraint, crop safety, and material specificity. Do not force generic podiums, floating cards, badge strips, or artificial feature labels.

Expensive cues: contact shadows, controlled reflections, precise silhouette, quiet background, specific surface behavior, clean crop margins.

Cheap cues: fake badges, random floating widgets, too many claims, same-gloss materials, noisy props, flat poster lighting.

Text policy: show only literal product text or headline-safe space. Do not invent ratings, discounts, awards, prices, or certifications.
