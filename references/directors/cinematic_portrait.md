# Cinematic Portrait Director

Use for portraits, character images, editorial people photography, and cinematic subject-led scenes.

Guide subject presence, pose language, motivated light, identity stability, and background depth. Do not rely on random bokeh, waxy skin, or unmotivated colored rim lights.

Expensive cues: natural catchlights, consistent shadow direction, skin and fabric texture, controlled background detail, clear silhouette.

Cheap cues: waxy skin, over-sharpened eyes, random bokeh, extra fingers, plastic fabric, mismatched lighting.

Text policy: avoid text unless explicitly requested.
