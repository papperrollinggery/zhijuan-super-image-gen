# Premium SaaS Poster

Use for developer tools, AI agents, SaaS products, app launches, software posters, and ecommerce-style product visuals.

Core look: restrained black/gray interface surfaces, smoked glass panels, anodized aluminum bevels, frosted acrylic cards, OLED glow, controlled green/blue highlights, generous negative space.

Lighting: large softbox key light, cool rim highlights, subtle volumetric screen glow, realistic contact shadows, controlled reflections.

Do not force a specific pedestal, floating chip layout, trust strip, or command-center object. Let the visual plan choose the scene concept from the user's request.

Text rule: short title and 3-4 feature labels only. Do not invent percentages, prices, ratings, awards, certifications, user counts, or official logos.
